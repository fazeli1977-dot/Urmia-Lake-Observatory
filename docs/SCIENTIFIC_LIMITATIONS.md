# Scientific Limitations & Honesty Policy

1. **Hypersaline Spectral Ambiguity:** Shallow brine sheets and wet halite crusts on Lake Urmia can cause spectral overlap in optical imagery; uncertainty bounds are mandatory.
2. **Bathymetric Shift:** Salt precipitation on the lakebed alters historical bathymetry; volume values are model-based estimates.
3. **GRACE Spatial Resolution:** Satellite gravimetry (~300 km footprint) reflects regional Total Water Storage anomalies, NOT point-scale aquifer levels.
4. **In-Situ Hydrometry:** Real-time domestic gauge/piezometer APIs are not publicly available; in-situ modules remain `Data Unavailable` unless validated user CSV/GeoJSON data is imported.
